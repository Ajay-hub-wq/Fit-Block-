## App Name: FIT BLOCK

## Short Description (80 chars):
Original 8x8 block puzzle - drag, fit, clear! Smooth, colorful & addictive.

## Long Description:
FIT BLOCK is a beautifully crafted original block puzzle game.

Drag colorful blocks onto the 8x8 board. Fill a row or column to clear it. Simple to learn, challenging to master!

FEATURES:
• Original design - rich colors, smooth 60 FPS
• 8x8 classic gameplay with modern feel
• Smart piece generator - always fair
• Combo system - chain clears for huge scores
• Coins & Gems - earn as you play
• Daily rewards - come back every day
• Offline play - no internet needed
• No ads interrupting gameplay
• Small size, works on all Android phones

Perfect for 2-minute breaks or hour-long sessions. Train your brain, relax your mind.

No data collection. No unnecessary permissions.
