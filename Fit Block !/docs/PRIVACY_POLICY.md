# Privacy Policy for FIT BLOCK

Last updated: 2026-08-09

FIT BLOCK is an offline-first puzzle game.

**Data Collection:**
- We do NOT collect personal information, contacts, location, camera, microphone.
- Game progress (best score, coins, gems, settings) is stored locally on device using Android DataStore.
- If ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization per Google's policy. You can opt out in device settings.

**Permissions:**
- INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: for ads (if enabled) and optional analytics
- VIBRATE: for haptic feedback (can be disabled in settings)
